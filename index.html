Topic Summary

Abandoned yet Unforgotten: Stories of Apollo 11 on the Moon In July 1969, humanity stepped onto the Moon for the first time, yet the journey did not end when the astronauts returned to Earth. In the silence of the Sea of Tranquility, a true space museum was left behind: scientific instruments such as seismometers and laser retroreflectors, messages of peace from seventy-three nations, and even the everyday tools that Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin left behind to lighten their return vessel. This thirteen-page digital comic transforms NASA's historical archives into a visual adventure designed for all audiences. Explore, page by page, the history behind these machines and silent mementos that still remain intact upon the lunar surface, demonstrating that although they were left behind in space, their scientific and human legacy remains more vibrant than ever.
